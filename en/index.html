Soft Wow Cabin

A mountain home in Grand Villard, Sauze d’Oulx, designed first to be lived in, then to be shared.

The philosophy

Designed around the landscape.

The cabin began with a simple idea: inside and outside should feel like parts of the same space.

Windows, mirrors and the large round opening bring light, trees and mountains indoors. The landscape changes through the day and across the seasons, and the cabin changes with it.

Soft Wow Cabin is in Grand Villard, a residential part of Sauze d’Oulx surrounded by greenery. A walk of about 15 minutes, essentially flat, takes you into the heart of the village.

Close enough to enjoy Sauze on foot, yet secluded enough that once you are home, the trees, light and mountains take over.

“Inside and outside become part of the same landscape.”
Grand Villard

A home within the landscape.

Here you can see the residential complex where Soft Wow Cabin is located, seen from Sauze d’Oulx: at the edge of the village, already surrounded by greenery with the mountains behind it. That short distance creates two different rhythms: the village on foot, and the feeling of returning to the trees when you come home.

Coming home

A large garden, four seasons.

The greenery is not a distant view: it is the setting the cabin sits in.

The building is surrounded by a large shared garden, among larches and fir trees. In winter it is covered in snow, in autumn it turns golden, and in summer it is your first encounter with the mountain as soon as you step outside.

The photograph shows the real setting of the apartment: residential, quiet and deliberately a little removed from the centre.

Living the cabin

Spaces that adapt to the people who live in them.

Every element has more than one purpose, without giving up visual calm. The cabin changes with the light, the seasons and the way it is lived in.

Bedroom

Quiet and bright, with custom okoumé furniture, a double bed and a premium 25 cm Falomo “Sbadiglio” mattress. A fold-away table creates, when needed, a place to study, work or read in front of the windows.

Kitchen

Fully equipped and designed for real cooking: Labradorite worktop, oven, dishwasher, kettle and toaster, together with the tools we use ourselves. Coffee has its own ritual, described just below.

Living area

The sofa bed Truman by Felis turns the living room into a sleeping area with a real 140 cm double mattress. An LG OLED TV and a Sonos Era 100 complete the space, each with its own role.

It is a real bed, and it is so comfortable that when we sleep here, it is actually my favourite.
Beyond the windows

One home, four seasons.

The balcony, fir trees, garden and mountains are constantly changing. In summer the greenery reaches the windows; in winter the snow redraws the entire scene.

Slow coffee philosophy

The coffee ritual.

Two ways to start the day.

When there is time, we start with the beans. The Sage Barista Touch grinds coffee fresh and guides you through espresso and milk-based drinks: a few extra minutes turned into a daily ritual.

Boutic Caffè · Maestri del Gusto, Turin We leave an initial supply of whole-bean coffee from Boutic Caffè, a historic artisan roastery from Turin. Careful origin selection, a focus on terroir and slow roasting: a philosophy Boutic sums up as “espressamente lento” and one that felt perfect for this cabin. Discover Boutic Caffè ↗
Nespresso · when simplicity matters Alongside the Sage there is also a Nespresso machine: quick and practical. You will find an initial supply of Nespresso and compatible Lavazza capsules, so your first coffee is already waiting.
After the mountain

Coming home.

The ski & bike room is designed to make coming home after a day outside simple.

Skis, boots, helmets and bikes can stay outside the apartment. The boot and shoe dryer helps keep your gear dry; an electrical socket is available for e-bikes. Come in, change, and leave the mountain gear organised in its own space.

Energy comfort

The building underwent an energy upgrade as part of the Superbonus 110% and is now rated Energy Class B. Insulation and upgraded windows improve comfort in every season; shared solar panels contribute to common-area energy use. Thermostat controls are explained in the practical guest guide.

Private covered parking

The covered parking space has a private EV wallbox. Charging is included for normal use during your stay; for particularly intensive charging needs, please contact us so we can agree on the best arrangement.

Sauze d’Oulx

Village and mountain.

Grand Villard lets you experience Sauze at two speeds: the village, restaurants and lifts within walking distance; greenery and quiet as soon as you head home. At sunset the village blends back into woods and mountains — the wider landscape the cabin opens onto in every season.

A base in the Alps

The mountains, all year round.

Soft Wow Cabin is a base for experiencing Sauze and the Upper Susa Valley in completely different seasons: snow and skiing in winter; trails, bikes and high-altitude roads when the green returns.

Winter · VialatteaExplore the interactive map ↗

Snow, forests and 400 km of skiing.

Sauze d’Oulx is one of the gateways to the Vialattea, the large international ski area linking Sauze, Sestriere, Sansicario, Cesana, Claviere, Pragelato and Montgenèvre in France.

Around 400 km of ski runs · over 100 km in the Sauze sector

From Sportinia, Sauze’s pistes connect into the wider ski area, through forests and high-altitude terrain. For anyone who prefers not to bring skis and boots home every day, Clotes Ski Center also offers heated ski storage directly by the Clotes chairlift.

Summer · Outdoor

Trails, MTB and alpine roads.

When the snow disappears, the same mountain becomes a landscape to explore: walking and hiking, MTB, e-bikes, downhill and enduro, through forests, valleys and high-altitude views.

Faure Sport and 3Sixty offer local bike and e-bike hire, as well as winter equipment.

Strada dell’Assietta

For anyone who loves two wheels, this is one of the area’s iconic routes: a spectacular high-altitude gravel road along the ridge between the Upper Susa Valley and Val Chisone, also a favourite with motorcyclists.

Bikers welcome

The mountains on two wheels.

Motorbikes, enduro, MTB or e-bikes: two wheels are not an afterthought here. The covered parking and the ski & bike room let you leave helmets and gear outside, change, and dry boots and shoes before entering the cabin.

It is a detail that matters to us: this cabin was also designed by someone who has experienced the mountains on two wheels.

Getting here

In the heart of the Alps, yet easy to reach.

Soft Wow Cabin is in Grand Villard, Sauze d’Oulx, about 10 km from Oulx. It is easy to reach by car, by train via Oulx, or by air via Turin Airport.

Coming home

Coming home, slowing down.

As evening falls outside, the cabin changes rhythm: warm light, wood, the forest beyond the windows, and Chaberton returning indoors as part of everyday life.

A collection created for Soft Wow Cabin

Three subjects. Three ways of telling the story of these mountains.

The collection was created to tell the story of this territory through three perspectives: culture, geography and landscape. Mollino, Chaberton and Lago Nero bring three different ways of reading these mountains into the cabin, becoming part of the design alongside okoumé wood, steel, forest green and natural light.
